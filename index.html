How a UPI payment travels

Four steps. Settlement times vary.

Step 1Scan the merchant QR or enter a UPI ID.
Step 2Your app sends a signed request on the UPI network.
Step 3Both banks verify accounts and authorisation.

For students

Digital Citizen Toolkit

Flip for tips, then quiz. Nothing is stored.

Never share OTP / PIN

Tap to flip

Banks and UPI apps will not ask for your OTP on a call. Hang up and verify in the official app.

Verify before you tap

Tap to flip

Hover or long-press links. Check the domain. When unsure, open the site yourself from a bookmark.

Strong passwords + 2FA

Tap to flip

Use a unique passphrase and turn on two-factor authentication wherever it is offered.

Report cyber fraud

Tap to flip

Call helpline 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in if money or data is at risk.

Respect others online

Tap to flip

No harassment, no hate. Screenshot less; empathy more.

Spot fake news

Tap to flip

Check the source, date, and a second trusted outlet before you share.
